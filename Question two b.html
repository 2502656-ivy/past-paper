Three Types of Lists Supported by HTML5

1. Unordered List (<ul>)

Displays items with bullet points. Use it when the order of items does not matter.

Scenario: A hospital website listing the services it offers.

Our Services

  • Outpatient
  • Maternity
  • Laboratory

2. Ordered List (<ol>)

Displays items with numbers or letters. Use it when the order or sequence of items matters.

Scenario: A website explaining the steps to pay a bill using M-Pesa.

How to Pay via M-Pesa

  1. Go to Lipa na M-Pesa
  2. Enter the Paybill number
  3. Enter the amount
  4. Enter your PIN

3. Description List (<dl>)

Displays terms together with their descriptions. <dt> holds the term and <dd> holds its description.

Scenario: A university website with a glossary of academic terms.

Glossary

Diploma
A qualification awarded after completing a course of study.
Semester
A period of study within an academic year.
